How it works

No setup, no manual tagging. The description already has everything TubeShelf needs.

1

Play a song

Open any song on YouTube or YouTube Music. A "Shelf It" button appears on the watch page.

2

Review the tags

TubeShelf parses Singer, Music Director, Film, Lyricist, and Language from the description. Edit anything before confirming.

3

Shelve it

Playlists are created or reused automatically and the song is added, skipping any it's already in.

Organized by what matters

Every song lands in the playlists you'd actually want to browse later.

Built to stay out of your way

Stateless backend, minimal permissions, nothing tracked.

✓Signs in with your Google account via Chrome's built-in identity. TubeShelf never sees your password.
✓No database. Requests are processed and forgotten, not logged or stored.
✓Only touches the video you click "Shelf It" on. No watch history, no subscriptions.
✓Open source. Read exactly what it does on GitHub.
